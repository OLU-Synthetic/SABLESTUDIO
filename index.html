FAQ

Short answers.

No agency theatre. No vague retainers. You’re buying a system.

What does $5–10k include?+

Typically: positioning + messaging, a minimal identity system, a rewritten homepage/services/about/contact, and a launch-ready structure (Squarespace-ready or custom static build).

Do you do ads / ongoing “marketing”?+

Only after the system is coherent. If your offer is unclear, ads just amplify confusion. We can add landing pages, offers, and outbound assets once the brand reads premium.

Squarespace or custom?+

Squarespace is fast and maintainable. Custom static (GitHub Pages) is faster and cleaner. The right choice depends on who maintains it and how often you publish.
